Automated tests

Manual checks (need a real file)

  1. Upload a landscape photo — no empty band at any zoom, in any direction.
  2. Upload a portrait photo — same.
  3. Drag fast, leave the frame, release outside — the drag must not stick.
  4. Zoom to 4× and back to 1× — what was centred stays centred.
  5. Save, then Adjust — reopens where you left it and still zooms back out (proves the original was kept, not the crop).
  6. Tab to the frame, use arrow keys — image nudges; Shift makes it coarser.
  7. Escape and clicking the scrim both close without saving.
  8. Try a PDF or a 40MB file — refused with a specific reason, no editor opens.
  9. A phone photo taken in portrait must not bake sideways (EXIF).
